## Gradients

Not used. Flat surfaces keep the dashboard calm and make streaming balances easier to read.

## Tone and voice

**Use:** direct, specific, number-forward, factual. Keep statements short and explain what each action does.  
**Avoid:** hype, urgency, vague superlatives, exclamation marks, and “unlock.”  
**Example:** “1,000 tUSD8 funded for 30 days. Withdraw earned tokens anytime.”

## Usage

- Use CSS theme variables; do not hardcode brand colors in components.
- Use a 4 px spacing grid and a maximum of three font weights per view.
- Keep balances and addresses in tabular monospace.
- Keep the dashboard dark-first; make light mode available through the theme toggle.
- Avoid decorative gradients, excessive glow, and animated effects that obscure transaction state.
